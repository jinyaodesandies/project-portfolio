Research

Rush: A 3D Computational Fluid Model of Neuro-Vascular Coupling

Carney Center for Brain Science, Brown University · Carney Summer Scholars (SREIP for VLANS)

Built a 3D computational fluid dynamics model of a neural–capillary system using SALOME and OpenFOAM to simulate brain temperature and fluid transfer, investigating neuro-vascular coupling as a mechanism for heat management and biochemical computing. Advised by Christopher I. Moore; funded by the National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke.
